Guessing Game

The triple 2, 4, 6 conforms to a simple rule that we have in mind. Your job is to find out what the rule is by proposing additional triples and learning whether or not they also conform to the rule.

Click here for full directions
The rule we have in mind is concerned with a relation between any three integers and not with their absolute magnitude, i.e. it is not a rule like all integers above (or below) 50, etc. Your aim is to discover the rule by agreeing with your partner on additional sets of three integers to write down. After you write down such a triple, we shall tell you whether or not it conforms to the rule. Remember that your aim is not simply to find triples which conform to the rule, but to discover the rule itself!

When you and your partner feel highly confident that you know what the rule is, and not before, you are to click the "announce rule" button. You will only have one chance to guess the rule!

Please note that every time you write down a triple, you will also be asked to state your working hypothesis as to what the rule might be. This does not equal to trying to guess the rule and it is only needed so that we can keep track of your thought processes along the way.

You have 20 minutes to find out what the rule is, but there is no limit on how many triplets you can test. Good luck!
